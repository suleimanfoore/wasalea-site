The float goes out on trust. The record shouldn't.

Money leaves before dawn

Float and cash go out to several agents every morning, often by phone, often in a hurry. By mid‑morning nobody can say precisely how much is out there.

Repayments come back in pieces

An agent repays part of yesterday against part of today. Matching those payments to the right deployment by hand is where the errors creep in.

Spreadsheets quietly drift

One shared workbook, several people, a formula someone dragged too far. The numbers stop agreeing and nobody can prove which version was right.

How it works

Two entries a day. That's the whole routine.

  1. 1

    Morning: record what went out

    Enter the float and cash deployed to each agent. That's the day's position set — no reconstructing it from memory later.

  2. 2

    Evening: record what came back

    Enter each repayment once. It settles automatically against the oldest open deployment first, so partial payments land in the right place without you doing the arithmetic.

  3. 3

    Any time: see where you stand

    Outstanding balances per agent, colour‑coded by how long the money has been out, plus a summary of what was deployed, collected and utilised over any date range.

Features

Built for how this business actually runs

Oldest‑first settlement

Repayments clear against the oldest outstanding deployment automatically, so an agent's debt ages the way it should and nothing sits uncleared by accident.

Outstanding balances, aged

One screen for who owes what, shaded by how long it has been owed — the overdue positions find you rather than the other way round.

Per‑agent performance

An executive summary for any date range: deployed, collected and utilisation per agent, so you can see who turns float over and who just holds it.

A full audit trail

Every deployment and collection is kept with its history. When a figure is questioned months later, there is a record to point at.

Safe for a team

Several people can have the books open at once. One person edits at a time and the app shows who that is, so two users can't overwrite each other's work.

Restore points

Saves build up a history you can look through and roll back to, with a preview of what a given point contains before anything is put back.

Tablet companion

The day's position, in the field

An Android tablet app that shows the same books, read‑only. Useful when you are out among the outlets and want to check a balance without carrying a laptop or risking an accidental edit. It reads; the desktop app remains the only place anything is entered.
