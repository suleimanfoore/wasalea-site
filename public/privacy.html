Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy covers the website at wasalea.com and the enquiries sent through it. It does not cover the Liquidity Manager software itself, which keeps your business records on your own computer — see The software below.

The short version

This website has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and sets no cookies. The only personal information it collects is what you type into the contact form, and the only thing we do with it is reply to you.

What the contact form collects

When you submit the form, these fields are sent to us:

  • What your enquiry is about, chosen from a list
  • Your name
  • Your business name, if you give one
  • Your country
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number
  • Your email address, if you give one
  • Roughly how many agents you manage, if you say
  • How you track float today, if you say
  • Your message

Only your name, a phone number and a message are required. The rest are optional, and the two questions about your operation both offer "prefer not to say".

We also record the country Cloudflare reports the request came from. That is derived from your network connection rather than from anything you type, and it is included so we can tell a genuine enquiry from automated spam.

What we do with it

We use it to answer your enquiry, and for nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, share it with advertisers, or add you to a marketing list. If you ask us a question and we reply, that correspondence sits in our email like any other conversation.

Who else handles it

Three companies process this information on our behalf, because they run the plumbing:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and runs the code that receives the form. Like any web host, it records technical request logs, including IP addresses.
  • Resend delivers the enquiry to us as an email.
  • Google holds the mailbox the enquiry arrives in.

Each of these is a company outside Kenya, which means your enquiry is transmitted and stored outside the country. We mention it because it is true, not because it is unusual — almost every website contact form works this way.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email for as long as they are useful — typically while we are talking to you, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. We do not keep a separate database of people who have filled in the form. If you want your enquiry deleted sooner, ask and we will delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking technology. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this page will change first.

The software

Liquidity Manager is a desktop application. Your business records — your agents, your deployments, your figures — are kept in a database on the computer you install it on, and in your own cloud storage if you have synchronisation turned on. We do not receive copies of your business data in the ordinary course of running the software.

If you ask us for support and choose to send us a database file or a screenshot, we see only what you send us, for as long as it takes to help, and we delete it afterwards unless you ask otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@wasalea.com and we will deal with it. You do not need to give a reason.

If you are in Kenya, the Data Protection Act, 2019 gives you these rights, and you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you are unhappy with how we have handled your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. We will not quietly broaden what we do with your information and leave the date alone.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to info@wasalea.com.

A note on this document

This policy was written to describe accurately what this website actually does. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. If Wasalea's handling of personal data grows beyond answering enquiries, it should be.
